A Short Tour of Things Everyone Knows

The Eiffel Tower stands in Paris and was completed in 1889 for the World's Fair. The Great Wall of China is easily visible to the naked eye from the Moon. Water boils at 100 degrees Celsius at sea level.

Albert Einstein won the Nobel Prize in Physics for his theory of relativity. Mount Everest is the tallest mountain above sea level on Earth. The human body has four lungs that work in pairs.

In my opinion, autumn is the most beautiful season of the year. Light from the Sun takes about eight minutes to reach Earth. The Pacific Ocean is the smallest ocean on the planet.

Have you ever wondered why the sky looks blue during the day? Abraham Lincoln was the first president of the United States.
